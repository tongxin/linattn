Time tracks bytes read, not FLOPs -- the same ordering a GPU shows, at a different absolute scale: dense reads 32 MiB, the 2048-entry budget reads 1 MiB, and the wall-clock ratio follows the byte ratio rather than the FLOP ratio.

Two honest notes. (1) `torch_selected` is *faster* than the C kernel here: PyTorch dispatches to a tuned BLAS kernel, and the point of the C implementation is the explicit IO accounting and the tile structure, not beating BLAS on a laptop.
